## 2026-06-11 Supplier Refresh Authoritative Cloud Pull

- Supplier management manual refresh now forces a Firestore server read for `suppliers` and `purchase_orders`.
- Refresh replaces the local supplier and purchase-order state with the cloud result, then writes the same result to the current store local cache.
- This prevents old B-terminal local supplier data from surviving after a supplier was deleted or changed in the cloud.
- Purchase orders refreshed from cloud are also written into `dataManager` purchases cache so financial supplier-debt calculations use the same refreshed data.
- Verified locally and online with Playwright: `zeng/123456` can open supplier management, click refresh, see sync time, and no page errors occur.

## 2026-06-11 Purchase Debt Versioning

- New purchase orders now carry `lastModified`, improving version-based merge behavior across terminals.
- Credit purchase supplier debt is recalculated from purchase orders instead of incrementing the current local supplier balance.
- This reduces the risk of a stale local supplier balance overwriting the correct cloud debt after multi-terminal use.
- Verified with TypeScript, production build, and local Playwright smoke test on the inventory purchase tab.
